# 📧 AI Sales Email Generator

## 📖 Overview

The AI Sales Email Generator is an intelligent sales automation workflow built with **n8n** and **OpenAI**.

It automatically generates personalized sales emails based on customer information, business requirements, and product offerings. The workflow uses AI to create professional outreach emails, stores generated content, and delivers ready-to-send sales communications.

---

## 🚀 Features

- AI-Powered Sales Email Generation
- Personalized Customer Outreach
- Product & Service Recommendations
- Professional Sales Copywriting
- Google Sheets Integration
- Google Drive Email Archive
- Automated Email Delivery
- End-to-End Sales Automation

---

## ⚙️ Workflow

```text
Customer Information
         │
         ▼
Webhook
         │
         ▼
AI Sales Email Generator
         │
         ▼
Generate:
• Personalized Greeting
• Sales Pitch
• Product Benefits
• Call-to-Action
• Professional Closing
         │
         ▼
Store Email Record
(Google Sheets)
         │
         ▼
Save Email Copy
(Google Drive)
         │
         ▼
Send Sales Email
```
