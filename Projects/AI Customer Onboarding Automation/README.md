# 🤝 AI Customer Onboarding Automation

## 📖 Overview

The AI Customer Onboarding Automation workflow is an intelligent business automation solution built with **n8n** and **OpenAI**.

It automates the complete customer onboarding process by analyzing customer information, classifying onboarding priority, generating a personalized welcome guide, storing customer records, and sending a professional AI-generated welcome email.

---

## 🚀 Features

- AI Customer Analysis
- Customer Priority Classification
- Customer Type Detection
- AI Business Summary Generation
- Google Sheets CRM Integration
- Google Drive Welcome Guide Creation
- Personalized AI Welcome Email
- End-to-End Customer Onboarding Automation

---

## ⚙️ Workflow

```text
Customer Registration
        │
        ▼
Webhook
        │
        ▼
AI Customer Analysis
        │
        ▼
Classify Customer
(Business / Priority / Summary)
        │
        ▼
Store Customer Record
(Google Sheets)
        │
        ▼
Generate Welcome Guide
(Google Drive)
        │
        ▼
Generate Personalized Email
(OpenAI)
        │
        ▼
Send Welcome Email
```
