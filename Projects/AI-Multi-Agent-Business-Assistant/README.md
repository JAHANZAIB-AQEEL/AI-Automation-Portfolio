# 🤖 AI Multi-Agent Business Assistant

## 📖 Overview

The AI Multi-Agent Business Assistant is an enterprise-grade automation workflow built with **n8n** and **OpenAI**. It coordinates multiple specialized AI agents to analyze different business functions, including Sales, Marketing, Finance, and Customer Support. The outputs are merged into a single executive report, which is automatically stored and delivered.

---

## 🚀 Features

- Multi-Agent AI Collaboration
- Sales Performance Analysis
- Marketing Insights
- Financial Analysis
- Customer Support Assistance
- AI Response Aggregation
- Executive Summary Generation
- Google Sheets Integration
- Google Drive Storage
- Automated Email Reporting

---

## ⚙️ Workflow

```text
Webhook
      │
      ▼
Business Coordinator AI
      │
      ├── Sales AI
      ├── Marketing AI
      ├── Finance AI
      └── Support AI
              │
              ▼
       Merge Responses
              │
              ▼
      Executive Summary AI
              │
      ┌───────┼────────┐
      ▼       ▼        ▼
Google   Google     Email
Sheets    Drive     Report
```
