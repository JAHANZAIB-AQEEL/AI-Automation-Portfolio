# 📊 AI Executive Business Reporting System

## 📖 Overview

The AI Executive Business Reporting System is an intelligent automation workflow built using **n8n** and **OpenAI**.

It collects business metrics, generates an AI-powered executive report, stores business data, and automatically emails a professional report to executives.

---

## 🚀 Features

- AI Executive Report Generation
- Business Performance Analysis
- Google Sheets Integration
- Google Drive Report Storage
- Professional Email Automation
- Webhook Trigger
- Automated Business Reporting

---

## 🛠 Technologies

- n8n
- OpenAI
- Google Sheets
- Google Drive
- Gmail / SMTP
- Webhooks

---

## 📈 Business Benefits

- Automates executive reporting
- Reduces manual report preparation
- Generates AI-powered business insights
- Stores reports securely
- Delivers reports automatically
