# 🧾 AI Invoice Generator

## 📖 Overview

The AI Invoice Generator is an intelligent business automation workflow built with **n8n** and **OpenAI**.

It automates invoice creation by processing customer and billing information, generating professional invoices, calculating totals, storing invoice records, creating downloadable invoice documents, and sending invoices directly to customers via email.

---

## 🚀 Features

- AI Invoice Generation
- Automatic Billing Calculation
- Customer Information Processing
- Invoice Number Generation
- Google Sheets Integration
- Google Drive PDF Storage
- Automated Invoice Email Delivery
- End-to-End Billing Automation

---

## ⚙️ Workflow

```text
Customer Order
        │
        ▼
Webhook
        │
        ▼
AI Invoice Processing
        │
        ▼
Generate:
• Invoice Number
• Customer Details
• Itemized Charges
• Total Amount
• Payment Summary
        │
        ▼
Store Invoice Record
(Google Sheets)
        │
        ▼
Generate Invoice
(Google Drive / PDF)
        │
        ▼
Send Invoice Email
```
