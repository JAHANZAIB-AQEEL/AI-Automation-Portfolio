# 👥 AI CRM Assistant

## 📖 Overview

The AI CRM Assistant is an intelligent customer relationship management automation workflow built with **n8n** and **OpenAI**.

It automatically captures customer information, analyzes customer interactions, classifies leads, generates AI-powered summaries, updates CRM records, creates customer reports, and sends personalized follow-up emails to improve customer engagement and sales efficiency.

---

## 🚀 Features

- AI Customer Analysis
- Lead Qualification
- Customer Segmentation
- AI Customer Summary
- Follow-up Recommendations
- Google Sheets CRM Integration
- Google Drive Report Generation
- Automated Email Follow-ups

---

## ⚙️ Workflow

```text
Customer Submission
        │
        ▼
Webhook
        │
        ▼
OpenAI Customer Analysis
        │
        ▼
Determine:
• Customer Type
• Lead Score
• Priority
• AI Summary
        │
        ▼
Update CRM
(Google Sheets)
        │
        ▼
Generate Customer Report
(Google Drive)
        │
        ▼
Send Follow-up Email
```
