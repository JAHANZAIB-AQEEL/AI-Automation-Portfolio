# 📈 AI Lead Capture & Google Sheets CRM

## 📖 Overview

The AI Lead Capture & Google Sheets CRM is an intelligent sales automation workflow built with **n8n** and **OpenAI**.

It automatically captures incoming leads, analyzes customer information using AI, qualifies lead quality, assigns lead priority, stores records in a CRM database, generates lead summaries, and sends automated notifications to the sales team.

---

## 🚀 Features

- AI Lead Qualification
- Customer Information Analysis
- Lead Priority Classification
- Sales Opportunity Scoring
- Google Sheets CRM Integration
- Google Drive Lead Report Generation
- Automated Sales Email Notifications
- End-to-End Lead Management

---

## ⚙️ Workflow

```text
Lead Submission
        │
        ▼
Webhook
        │
        ▼
AI Lead Analysis
        │
        ▼
Determine:
• Lead Quality
• Priority
• Customer Type
• AI Summary
        │
        ▼
Store Lead
(Google Sheets CRM)
        │
        ▼
Generate Lead Report
(Google Drive)
        │
        ▼
Notify Sales Team
(Email)
```
