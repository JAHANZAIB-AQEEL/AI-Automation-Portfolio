# 📅 AI Meeting Scheduler

## 📖 Overview

The AI Meeting Scheduler is an intelligent scheduling automation workflow built with **n8n** and **OpenAI**.

It automates the meeting scheduling process by analyzing meeting requests, organizing participant information, selecting preferred dates and times, generating meeting details, storing schedules, and sending personalized meeting invitations and confirmations.

---

## 🚀 Features

- AI Meeting Scheduling
- Intelligent Date & Time Coordination
- Participant Management
- Meeting Agenda Generation
- Google Sheets Integration
- Google Drive Meeting Records
- Automated Email Invitations
- End-to-End Meeting Scheduling Automation

---

## ⚙️ Workflow

```text
Meeting Request
        │
        ▼
Webhook
        │
        ▼
AI Meeting Analysis
        │
        ▼
Generate:
• Meeting Title
• Date & Time
• Participants
• Agenda
• Meeting Summary
        │
        ▼
Store Meeting Record
(Google Sheets)
        │
        ▼
Generate Meeting Details
(Google Drive)
        │
        ▼
Send Meeting Invitation
```
